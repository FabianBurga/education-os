# M29 continuity map — discovered flows and candidate gaps

Status: discovery baseline; candidate M29 work is not implemented. A “gap”
means no matching end-to-end linkage was verified in the inspected repository,
not that the underlying domain capability is absent.

| Flow | Source authority → destination | Current capability / evidence | Missing link to evaluate | Permission/event/human boundary | M29 action required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teacher → Student | Teacher Console → M1/M2/M3; M18/M21 | Assigned roster, attendance, grades, alerts; M28 adds enrollment/placement continuity. | Unified owned follow-up across teacher task, M21 action and M27 obligation. | Current assignment + domain permission; canonical source event; teacher performs command. | Map current owned tasks and decide whether an explicit cross-reference is needed. |
| Student → Teacher | Student Console/domain facts → teacher roster/alerts | Student attendance/results/progress and teacher alerts exist. | No proven student-originated response channel tied to an owner/obligation. | Authenticated student scope; no broad teacher access inferred. | Assess whether existing pending/task concepts suffice; do not create chat by default. |
| Teacher → Family | Staff-authored M13 communication → M6/M12 notice | Explicit target, preview, publish and in-app delivery to active grants. | Link reason/context to operational follow-up without copying restricted evidence into message. | Communications permissions, active portal grant, human publication, audit/outbox. | Reuse M13 and assess safe context references/recipient-purpose semantics. |
| Family → Teacher | Family notice acknowledgement → receipt/telemetry | Family can acknowledge a notice; delivery report derives receipt state. | General reply/response and routing to an accountable teacher/owner not verified. | Current guardian identity + active student grant; acknowledgement scoped to notice. | Define whether response is required and where ownership belongs before proposing persistence. |
| Institution → Family | Family-targeted notice/communication | M13 supports scoped targets, staff authoring and in-app delivery. | Consent/preferences, urgency/freshness and escalation policy not proven complete. | Staff publish permission; resolved recipients restricted by active grant. | Inventory institution policy and decide only evidence-backed missing semantics. |
| Family → Institution | Family acknowledgement; family/admin relationship data | Notice acknowledgement and explicit grant administration exist. | General family requests/documents/support intake not verified. | Guardian portal grant; institution routes to authorized staff. | Discovery only; do not assume CRM/helpdesk requirement. |
| Intervention → Family | M21 intervention/follow-up → M13 communication | M21 follow-up types include `FAMILY_CONTACT`; M13 can target families. | No verified first-class intervention-to-communication or response-to-follow-up link. | Intervention sensitivity and read permissions; separate human decision to communicate. | Identify minimal opaque references; never expose intervention wholesale. |
| Follow-up → Family | M21 actions/follow-ups; M27 obligations → notice | M21 actions/follow-ups and M27 obligations exist independently. | No proven generated, owned family-contact obligation tied to delivery/response. | M21 owns execution follow-up; M27 owns accountability obligation; distinct IDs/state. | Keep ownership separate; propose bridge only if measured gap requires it. |
| Family response → Accountability | Receipt/response → M27 case/obligation | M27 cases/reviews/obligations and M13 recipient receipts exist. | Acknowledgement/response is not proven to close or update M27 obligation automatically. | Human M27 decisions remain explicit; M18 provenance. | Define evidence reference and human review; prohibit implicit decision mutation. |
| Offline teacher → family | M20 sync → M3 → M18 → consumers → M13 | M20 sync mutates authoritative attendance and emits events; M28 validates M18/M21 continuity. | No verified family dispatch from offline client before/after reconciliation; no M20-to-communication contract. | Server reauthorizes current teacher scope; only server-side authorized publish after sync. | Reuse chain; prohibit client-side direct send or second offline queue. |
| Attendance → family | M3 attendance → family views/notices | Guardian attendance read exists; M13 can send family notice. | No evidence of event-triggered family notice policy, intentionally avoiding automatic disclosure. | Family grant and staff policy/human publication. | Decide if any bounded human-initiated continuity is needed; no automatic absence alerts by default. |
| Results → family | M3 grades → student/guardian read | Guardian/student grade endpoints expose authorized results; M28 minimized audit/event feedback. | No proven correction notice/ack workflow tied to published-result lifecycle. | Existing portal visibility; feedback is sensitive and omitted from audit/event payload. | Review visibility/publishing semantics and correction notification need. |
| Timeline → family | M18/M21 derived timeline → family | Staff/student timeline read exists; family APIs expose curated overview/attendance/grades instead. | No family-safe allowlist projection verified. | Never expose M21 timeline wholesale; explicit family data minimization. | Keep current curated views; define allowlist only if justified. |
| M27 accountability → teacher/family obligations | M27 cases/ownership/reviews/obligations → assigned staff / M13 | M27 human accountability queues and obligations exist; family contact in M21 follow-up vocabulary. | No unified teacher-family overdue/continuity view proven. | M27 decisions remain human, role scoped; restricted case evidence stays internal. | Evaluate cross-reference and aggregate metrics with denominators, not duplicate engine. |

Candidate missing links are hypotheses for contract review, not permission to
add tables, routes, automation or new domain state in M29-0.
